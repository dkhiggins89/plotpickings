PlotPickings

Harvest Tracker for Android

PlotPickings is a harvest tracker and garden journal for allotments and veg patches. Log what you pick, keep crop notes and costs, and compare one season with the next.

Get it on Google PlayFree to download

What it does

Log your harvests

Choose a crop, enter the weight or count and save. Kilos, pounds or items.

Crop records

Varieties, beds, sowing and planting dates, photos and dated notes.

Garden spending

Track seeds, plants, compost and tools, against a crop or the whole plot.

What it was worth

Add a shop price to estimate the value of everything you grew.

Compare seasons

See how each crop and each year stacks up, and share your season.

Use it at the plot

Works offline with weekend harvest reminders.
