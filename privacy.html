Your allotment records

Crop records, harvests, spending, planting notes and photos stay in the app's private device storage. No app account is required. We do not operate a records server or upload your garden records to advertising services. The app does not request contacts, microphone, camera or precise-location access. You select individual photos and files through Android's file picker.

Android cloud backup and automatic device transfer are disabled. Export a ZIP backup before moving phones or uninstalling. ZIP exports include your records and referenced photos; CSV exports include text records. You choose the destination. A storage or cloud provider you choose has its own policies. Restoring a ZIP replaces the current records after confirmation. Purchases and ad-consent settings are not included in backups.

Deleting a crop also removes its linked harvests, spending and notes after confirmation. Deleted or replaced photos may remain in private storage until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled; unreferenced photos are excluded from exports. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local records and settings, but not exports you saved elsewhere. We hold no server copy.

Advertising and choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Small banners may appear while browsing. Banners are suspended during editors, dialogs, file operations and while the app is in the background. We do not use interstitial, rewarded or app-open ads. All notebook tools work offline and remain available after optional advertising consent is declined. An active ad-removal purchase stops banners.

Google's ads SDK can collect and share IP/address information, approximate location inferred from IP, interaction events, performance diagnostics and device or applicable account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google encrypts SDK network data in transit. We add no separate analytics SDK. Crop names, notes, harvests, spending and photos are not provided as ad targeting information.

Where required, Google's messages provide consent, refusal and management choices. Reopen Ad privacy choices under More > Ads & purchases when that entry point is required. Some limited ads may still be permitted after optional purposes are refused. Android settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Google and its disclosed partners apply their own retention and privacy policies.

Google ads information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Optional permanent ad removal

Google Play handles the one-time ad-removal purchase. It is not a subscription. Play provides the price and confirms it at checkout. We do not receive card details. The app receives signed purchase information, product IDs, states and tokens to check ownership, acknowledge payment and restore access. These are not sent to a publisher server or logged by this app. Only the entitlement flag is saved in private preferences; full receipts are not stored.

Purchases and restoration require the purchasing Google account and a Play connection. A previously confirmed entitlement works offline. A later successful ownership check can update entitlement following a refund or revocation. Clearing app data removes the local flag; Restore purchase checks Play again. Backups do not transfer purchases. Google retains its transaction records under its policies. Removing ads stops banners; Play may still handle purchase or restoration requests.

Contact and updates

Contact fitandemapp@gmail.com about this app or policy. Delete local records and exported copies where you saved them. Use Google's account and privacy controls for data it handles. We may revise this policy as features change; an offline copy is included in the app.
